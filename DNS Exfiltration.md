**How DNS Exfiltration Works** Attackers encode data into [[DNS]] queries (subdomains), effectively tunnelling other protocols like [[HTTP]] over [[DNS]]. This is sometimes called "TCP over DNS." DNS tunnelling establishes a continuous communication channel for sending and receiving data.

> _Note: Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN) cannot be more than 255 characters and subdomains are capped at 63 characters_
